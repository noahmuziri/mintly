What Leaf does

Everything you need to read, question and decide.

Summarize

Add articles and ask what they say. Leaf pulls out the key facts, the evidence for the company and the reasons for caution.

Challenge

Ask for the strongest case for, or against. Leaf argues it as its supporters or critics would, then asks which risk would hurt most.

Compare

See where your sources agree and disagree, and get nudged to find one that disagrees with you.

Flag what's missing

Leaf points out what you haven't checked: how the price compares with earnings, who the biggest customers are.

Help with writing

Edits appear right in your argument. Tighten wording, add evidence or check for missing risks. You decide what to accept.

Check the price

Ask what a stock trades at and Leaf shows the quote, then reminds you a price alone doesn't say if it's cheap.
