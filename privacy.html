Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Mintly teaches teenagers how to think about money, using practice money only. This page explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We wrote it to be read.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, role (student, parent or teacher) and a password. Passwords are stored only as a salted hash, so we can't read them. If you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google.
  • Learning activity (students): which lessons you've started and finished, your quiz and activity answers, anything you type into lesson boxes, and your simulation decisions: the company, Buy/Sell/Wait choice, your written reasoning, your confidence and your Decision Score.
  • Links between accounts: a parent who enters a student's code, or a student who joins a teacher's class code, creates a link. Both sides can see it.
  • Technical data: a session cookie that keeps you signed in, and basic server logs. Your light/dark theme choice is saved in your own browser.

We don't collect payment details (billing isn't live), precise location, contacts, or anything from your device beyond the above.

Who can see what

  • Parents linked by a student's code can see that student's lesson progress and simulation decisions, including the reasoning they wrote.
  • Teachers can see the progress and simulation scores of students in classes the student joined.
  • Students can leave a class, and parents or teachers can remove a link, at any time.
  • No one else sees your learning activity. We do not sell personal information, show ads, or share data with advertisers.

Service providers

We use Google only if you choose "Continue with Google", an email service to send confirmation and password-reset emails, and Alpha Vantage for stock prices (we send it a ticker symbol, never your information). Hosting providers store our data on our behalf.

Young people

Mintly is for people aged 13 and over. Students under 18 should use Mintly with a parent or guardian's permission. If you're a parent and believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your choices

  • From your account page you can change your name and password, download all the data we hold about you, or delete your account. Deleting removes your profile, progress and decisions.
  • Parents and guardians can ask us to review or delete their child's data by contacting us.

How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete it, it's removed from our live systems.

Security

Passwords are hashed, sessions use secure cookies, and access to student data is limited to the student and the parents and teachers they're linked to. No system is perfectly secure; if something goes wrong that affects you, we'll tell you.
