Privacy

What Mintly counts, and what it never does.

Plain-language summary of how this site treats learner activity.

What we count, only if you say yes

When you tap "Okay" on the privacy notice, Mintly records simple activity: which lessons you open, which films you play, quiz scores, which Leaf topic categories you ask about (like "saving" or "scams"), and which preset interests you pick on Explore. We use this to see which lessons help and where learners get stuck.

What we never store

  • What you type. Your messages to Leaf, your answers in lessons and your written reasoning stay in your own browser. They are never sent to us.
  • Your name or email alongside activity. Activity is tied to a random ID that lives in your browser, not to who you are.
  • Payment details. Mintly doesn't collect card numbers.

Your choices

  • Choose "No thanks" and nothing is sent. The site works the same.
  • Change your mind any time by clearing this site's data in your browser, which also resets your random ID.
  • If you're under 13, ask a parent or guardian before saying yes.

Who can see it

Only Mintly's administrators can see activity, and they see anonymous learner IDs with topics and lesson progress. Parents see their own teen's weekly summary, never their private chats with Leaf.

Questions or deletion requests

Contact the Mintly team using the details provided with your plan, and include your learner ID (clear your browser data to reset it).
