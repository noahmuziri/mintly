Questions people ask

Is any real money involved?

No. Every simulation uses pretend money, so teens can make mistakes that cost nothing.

What's free?

Lesson 1 with its film and quiz, three questions for Leaf, and the demo. No account needed.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Will I be charged today?

Not yet. Billing isn't switched on in this preview, so choosing a plan opens an account with full access and nothing is charged.

We're a school. What do we get?

The School plan gives every student the full course and gives you a class dashboard to assign lessons and see who's stuck. It starts at 50 students.
