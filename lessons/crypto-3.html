Cryptocurrencies · Lesson 3

Should you? A way to decide

About 12 minutes · 4 activities · Your answers and progress are saved to your account

Mintly will never tell you to buy or not to buy crypto. What it can do is give you a way to think. This lesson gives you a simple set of questions to use on crypto, or on anything else people are excited about.

Warm-up · Typing

What would make you trust a money tip?

A person online says a coin is about to take off. What would you want to know about them, and about the coin, before believing them?

Five questions before any decision

  1. Do I understand it? Can I explain what it is and how it makes money in a few sentences? If not, wait.
  2. What's the worst case? If it went to zero, would that hurt me? Only use money you can afford to lose.
  3. Who benefits if I buy? Influencers, groups and exchanges often profit when you join. That doesn't make them wrong, but it should make you careful.
  4. What would change my mind? Decide in advance what evidence would make you sell or stay out, so excitement doesn't decide for you.
  5. Is it legal and allowed for me? In most places you must be 18 to use a crypto exchange, so it's a conversation to have with a parent.

Rules and taxes

Rules about crypto vary by country and change often. In many places, gains from selling crypto count as taxable income, and exchanges must follow identity rules. Any real decision should include an adult and up-to-date advice for where you live.

Hype, fear and FOMO

FOMO (noun)

"Fear of missing out". The pressure to jump into something because everyone else seems to be getting rich. It makes people buy near the top.

Slow, boring decisions usually beat fast, exciting ones. If you feel rushed, that's a signal to pause, not to act.

Leaf asks Think of a time you wanted something only because everyone else had it. What would you ask yourself next time, before spending money?
