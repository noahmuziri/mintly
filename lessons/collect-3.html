Collectables · Lesson 3

Buying, selling and flipping safely

About 13 minutes · 5 activities · Your answers and progress are saved to your account

Buying something for $50 and selling it for $80 sounds like an easy $30. Then the fees, shipping and your time show up. This lesson shows how reselling really works, how to work out your profit honestly, and how to stay safe doing it.

Warm-up · Typing

Is a $30 gap really $30 profit?

You buy an item for $50 and sell it online for $80. How much profit do you think you made? What could shrink it?

Where the money really goes

The difference between what you paid and what you sold for is not your profit. Costs eat into it:

  • Platform fees: marketplaces often take a percentage of the sale, and payment services take a little more.
  • Shipping and packaging: you may pay to send it, plus the box and padding.
  • Returns and problems: a refund or damaged package can wipe out the gain.
  • Your time: hunting, photographing, listing and answering questions. Ten hours for $20 is under $2 an hour.

Selling safely

  • Check the rules: many marketplaces and payment apps require you to be 18, so ask a parent to help you use theirs.
  • Keep conversations on the platform. Moving to a private chat or sending money through a friend-to-friend payment removes your protection.
  • Watch for overpayment scams (from lesson 9): a "buyer" sends too much and asks for a refund of the difference before their payment bounces.
  • Meet in public with an adult for local sales, and never share your address with strangers.

Taxes and records

Money you make from selling for a profit can count as income, depending on where you live and how much you make. Keep a simple record of what you paid, what you sold for and your costs, and ask a parent about whether anything is owed.

Key words from this lesson

Flipping
Buying something to resell quickly for more.
Platform fee
A cut of each sale taken by the marketplace.
Profit margin
How much you keep compared with what you paid.
Buyer protection
Payment protection if something goes wrong.
Overpayment scam
A fake buyer overpays and asks for a refund.
Records
Notes on what you paid, sold and spent.
