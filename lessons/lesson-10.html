Build your own money plan

About 16 minutes · 2 activities · Your answers are saved in this browser

You now know how to budget, save, use a bank, borrow carefully, let time grow money, read a paycheck and dodge scams. This final lesson puts it all together into one thing you can actually use: your own money plan.

A plan isn't a rulebook that stops you having fun. It's a way to decide ahead of time, so the choices you make in the moment line up with what you really want.

Five moves

  • 1. Know what comes in. Count your monthly income: allowance, a job, gifts.
  • 2. Split it. A simple starting point is 50% needs, 30% wants and 20% savings. Adjust it to fit your life.
  • 3. Pick a goal. Something specific, with a price and a date.
  • 4. Automate saving. Move the savings amount the day you're paid.
  • 5. Review monthly. Compare the plan with what really happened, then adjust.

Protect the plan

  • Build an emergency fund first. A small cushion keeps a surprise from derailing everything.
  • Avoid high-interest debt. It works against every other goal.
  • Watch for scams. One bad decision can erase months of saving.
  • Use the 24-hour rule for anything unplanned.
Leaf asks Look at your split. If something unexpected cost you $40 next month, which category would you take it from, and what would you give up? Naming that now makes the decision easier later.
