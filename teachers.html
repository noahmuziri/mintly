For Teachers

Teach how to think about money. See it happen.

Run Mintly with your class. Students work through the lessons and practise decisions with play money, and you see who has done what, with no grading to set up.

How it works in your classroom

1 minute

1. Create a class

Sign up as a teacher and create a class. You get a short join code.

1 minute

2. Students join

Students make an account, enter the code on their dashboard, and start Lesson 1.

Ongoing

3. Follow progress

Your dashboard shows completion for each student and each lesson, and who was active this week.

Practice money

4. Discuss decisions

Use the Decision Scores as a starting point: ask why a student chose to buy, sell or wait.

No real money. No ads. Students can leave your class at any time.

Mintly is free while in early access. Schools with 50+ students will have a School plan at $8 per student per month.
