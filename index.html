Good questions

Things parents (and teens) ask.

Is any real money involved?

No. Every simulation uses pretend money. Moving to real investing is a conversation for you and your teen, and Mintly never pushes it.

Does Leaf tell my teen what to buy?

Never. Leaf won't say buy or sell. It asks questions until your teen can explain their own reasoning, which is the whole point.

What ages is it for?

Mintly is written for 13 to 18. The lessons use plain language and real examples: paychecks, scams, sneakers, student loans.

What do parents get to see?

A weekly view of lessons finished and how their teen's reasoning is growing, plus a few conversation starters. Not their private chats with Leaf.

Can a whole class use it?

Yes. The School plan gives every student the full course and gives the teacher a roster, assignments and a skill heatmap.

What can I try without paying?

Lesson 1 with its film and quiz, three questions for Leaf, and the demo. No account needed.
